how it works

Three steps. No setup tax.

Open the app, add a pair of shoes, run. Soledger does the bookkeeping in the background.

Step 01

Add your shoes

Brand, model, what you paid, where you run them. Set the expected lifetime. Soledger starts with a science-backed default — adjust it to whatever your legs and the rubber agree on.

Step 02

Pick which pair

Soledger reads the run from Apple Health and asks: which shoes? One tap and the mileage is on the books. Bulk-assign older runs in a single sync review.

Step 03

Know when it's time

A quiet green ring while you've got life left. A warm amber when it's time to replace. The shoe earns its mileage; you see what it cost.

questions

The honest answers.

Where do my runs come from?
Apple Health. If your watch, treadmill, or another running app writes workouts to Apple Health, Soledger can see them. You'll be asked to grant read-only access to running workouts the first time you open the app. We don't request anything else.
Does Soledger collect any data about me?
No. Soledger has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics SDK. Your shoes and your runs are stored locally on your iPhone. We can't see them, and we don't want to. See the Privacy Policy for the long version.
How do you decide a shoe needs replacement?
Soledger defaults to a research-backed lifetime based on biomechanics studies — roughly 300–500 miles for standard daily trainers, the range at which peer-reviewed research consistently shows meaningful cushioning loss. Trail and indoor shoes get their own defaults tuned to their surfaces. But you're in charge: when you add a pair you can adjust the expected lifetime and a nudge threshold — the miles remaining when Soledger turns amber and sends a replace soon alert. The default nudge is 75 miles out. You can also retire a pair at any time — Soledger never forces it.
What does cost per mile actually mean?
It's the price you paid divided by the miles you've run in them. A $160 shoe that lasts 500 miles costs $0.32 per mile. A $250 carbon-plate racer you only run for short races might land at $1.50. Cost per mile isn't a number to feel bad about — it's a number to know, so you can pick the next pair with eyes open.
Does it work on iPad or Apple Watch?
iPhone only for now. Apple Watch and iPad support are planned for a future version.
Is there a subscription?
Soledger is free to download. Core tracking is free — no time limits, no nags.
What if I want my data out?
CSV export is supported for all your shoes and runs. Your runs already live in Apple Health regardless of Soledger.
